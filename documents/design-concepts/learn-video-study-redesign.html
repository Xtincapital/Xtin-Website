The quiet power of compounding

⏱ 7 min read◷ Includes calculator✎ 1 quiz question

Why compounding wins

When your returns start earning their own returns, growth stops being a straight line and starts to curve upward. A ₹5,000 monthly SIP doesn't feel like much in year one. By year twenty, the money made by your money is larger than everything you put in.

💡
The rule of 72Divide 72 by your annual return to estimate how many years it takes to double. At 12%, that's about 6 years.

The cost of waiting

Starting five years late doesn't cost you five years of contributions. It costs you the last five years of growth, which are the biggest ones. Two people investing the same ₹5,000 a month at 12% end up roughly ₹24 lakh apart after 25 years, just from a five-year delay.

⚠
Returns aren't guaranteedEquity returns swing year to year. Use 10 to 12% for planning, not as a promise.
