Frequently Asked Questions

How are tokens calculated? ▼
Tokens are chunks of text used by Claude. Xtin Journal sends compact trade context and deducts the actual input plus output tokens reported by the API.
Can I use AI after tokens finish? ▼
No. The AI coach is blocked when the balance reaches zero. Recharge any pack to continue.
How are payments handled? ▼
Payments are created and verified through Razorpay. Tokens are credited only after the backend verifies the Razorpay signature.
Is there a monthly minimum? ▼
No monthly minimum. Credits are prepaid and remain in the wallet until used.
How do I monitor my usage? ▼
The AI Credits tab shows available, purchased, and used tokens.
Can I set a budget limit? ▼
Yes. The fixed balance is the budget limit. AI usage pauses automatically when tokens reach zero.
